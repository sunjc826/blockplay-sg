# Pulau Ubin and the Changi bumboat crossing

Pulau Ubin is the twenty-third playable district. The authored island has an
irregular shoreline surrounded by sea, a timber landing, kampong-inspired
village, winding dirt-road loop, granite quarry, forest lookout, wetland
boardwalk and a visitor cottage. Eleven stamps and ten sectors connect those
places. This is a compressed game landscape, not a surveyed island or route map.

## Travel in the game

In Open World, travel to Changi and follow the Pulau Ubin route marker to the
Changi Point landing at **X 267, Z 180**. Approach on foot. The HUD shows
**T · Board bumboat to Pulau Ubin** within four metres. Press T, or tap the
boarding prompt on touch, to cross. The existing living-player, on-foot,
ground-level and proximity guards apply. The boat is scenery at a fast-travel
crossing, not a newly driveable vehicle or a real-time sailing simulation.

You arrive at **X 0, Z 193** on the island’s village jetty. Walk south to
**X 0, Z 205** and board the return bumboat to Changi. Arrivals face inland and
sit twelve metres beyond the return trigger, so they cannot bounce back
immediately. The crossing is free in the game; your health, armour, ammunition
and carried equipment travel with you. Supplies retain their existing seeded
contents across return visits. No fares, queues, schedules or ticket sales are
simulated. The normal district selector can also start an expedition on Ubin.

The new mainland landing is a separate, signed Changi Point/Changi Village
counterpart at the edge of the existing Changi map. Airport-to-village distances
are compressed. Its new geometry has no accepted photographic-reference claim;
Changi’s existing reviewed features remain scoped to their previous evidence.

## The island

The village has low timber/plaster houses, pitched roofs, provision-store
frontages and decorative bicycle racks. The shipped driving mode and motor
pool still use the existing car and helicopter; there is no bicycle rental
mechanic. A provision vendor sells the existing kaya-toast item and a jetty
guide explains the crossing. A separate forest clearing holds the FPS range.

The road loop and western spur leave every displayed road drivable. Outside
shore spans, corner water, the quarry pool and wetland mud exclude ground
movement, with an actual gap for the jetty and clear banks around the wetlands.
The connected quarry lookout has two stair flights, a lower landing at 3.2 m
and an upper platform at 6.4 m. This is an authored lookout, not a reconstruction
of the Bukit Puaka trail. Mangrove trunks and exposed roots stand inside the
wetland pool; timber paths form a continuous circuit around it.

The district uses medium risk, tier 2 loot, a four-bot mixed patrol preset,
eight tactical supplies and the existing bounded open-world battlefield.
Coastal ambient effects are enabled; it is not classified as an inland
paratrooper district. No new naval-assault landing sites were invented.

## Real-world access and research

In real life, boats leave **Changi Point Ferry Terminal beside Changi Village**,
at 51 Lorong Bekukong. NParks describes an on-demand bumboat crossing of about
ten minutes, typically S$4 per passenger each way, with departures usually
waiting for twelve passengers. Operators manage the service privately; confirm
the fare with the operator. These real arrangements are not game mechanics.

Research checked on 31 October 2026:

- [NParks: Visit Pulau Ubin](https://pulau-ubin.nparks.gov.sg/visitubin/), for
  mainland access, the village context and the island’s quarry history.
- [NParks: Nature-based recreation](https://pulau-ubin.nparks.gov.sg/nature-based-recreation/),

- [MPA: Public landing points](https://www.mpa.gov.sg/port-marine-ops/operations/port-infrastructure/landing-points),
  for Changi Point’s role serving the northern offshore islands.

## Verification

Shared district tests check real roads, water exclusion, car-width access to
stamps/anchors, target sightlines, vehicles, capture sites, supply placement,
upper-floor navigation, cover labels, batching and disposal. Ferry regressions
require Changi to be the island’s only mainland graph link and validate both
landing/arrival positions. Browser checks exercise actual T-input round trips,
state carry, Ubin gameplay and the mobile layout. Local receipts and images are
in `.cache/pulau-ubin/`.
