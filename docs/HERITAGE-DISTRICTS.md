# Little India and Katong / Joo Chiat

These are the twenty-first and twenty-second playable districts, following
Yishun. Both are authored, compressed heritage neighbourhoods with researched
landmark selection and schematic geometry. No new paid imagery, model, sound or
voice requests were made. Their empty `referenceFeatures` lists make no claim
of accepted photographic evidence. Neither has a reviewed learning catalog.

## Little India

The scene combines a Tekka-inspired market with an open central aisle and a
public gallery at 4.8 m reached by two stairs; a colourful villa inspired by the
House of Tan Teng Niah; a Campbell Lane pedestrian apron facing a faceted
Indian Heritage Centre-inspired building; a tiered, painted temple gateway and
forecourt; and low retail terraces with flower and textile details. Gateway
ornament is abstract, not a reproduction of specific religious figures.

The main road, pedestrian lane, closed building rooms and continuous sheltered
arcades have separate geometry and collision. Eleven stamps connect the stops.
Nine sectors distinguish the market, villa, lane, forecourt, shops and green.
A market vendor sells the existing curry-puff item; a local guide points out
paths. This is medium-risk game territory with normal medium heat, four mixed
patrol bots in its preset, tier 2 loot and nine tactical supplies. The shared
battlefield roster remains authoritative. Routes lead to Kampong Glam and
Orchard Road, with independently authored return arrivals.

## Katong / Joo Chiat

Facing Koon Seng-inspired pastel terrace rows have pilasters, arched window
mouldings, louvred shutters, tiled details and clay-coloured pitched roofs.
Covered five-foot ways run in front of the closed rooms; separate rear service
lanes offer alternative routes. A Red House-inspired corner, East Coast food
row and raised residential porch add different frontage types. An authored
neighbourhood pavilion has a public veranda at 3.6 m with two stair entries.
The public pavilion is generic; it does not imply access to real private homes.

There is no invented waterfront beside the conserved houses. The eastern green
provides a clear practice range and vehicle departure space. Eleven stamps,
ten sectors, a food vendor selling existing kaya toast and a local guide use
the shared systems. This is low-risk territory with normal low heat, three
assault patrol bots in its preset, tier 1 loot and seven tactical supplies.
Its reversible routes lead to Geylang and Changi.

## Shared integration and provenance

Both districts support walking, driving, FPS practice with eight targets,
car/helicopter spawns, seven capture sites, two NPCs, food and tactical supplies,
upper-floor loot, minimaps, deployment choices and connected-world travel. They
use existing combat, economy and equipment systems. Yishun retains its separate
fictional high-threat tuning.

All distances, street alignments, dimensions and playable access are compressed
and authored. Landmark names identify stylised counterparts. Selection and
architectural vocabulary were checked on 31 October 2026 against:

- [Singapore Tourism Board: Little India](https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/little-india/),
  for the market, villa and temple landmarks.
- [NHB: Little India Heritage Trail](https://www.roots.gov.sg/nhb/little-india-heritage-trail),
  for the precinct's retail and heritage context.
- [URA: Little India Conservation Area](https://www.ura.gov.sg/conservation/find-a-building/conservation-portal/ltin/),
  for its street and market character.
- [SG101: Heritage shophouses in Katong](https://www.sg101.gov.sg/resources/archives/heritage-shophouses-in-katong/),
  for pastel facades, raised houses and covered five-foot ways.
- [STB: Koon Seng Road shophouses](https://www.visitsingapore.com/ja_jp/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/katong-joo-chiat/shophouses-koon-seng-road/),
  for the conserved terrace streetscape.

## Verification

The shared district tests exercise car-width road, spawn, stamp, anchor and
checkpoint reachability; target sightlines; vehicle departure/exit clearance;
capture-site placement; measured cover; upper-floor walking support; supply
placement; geometry batching and disposal. Cover bands are measured from the
actual scene: the Tekka market, temple forecourt and Katong five-foot-way sector
are broken cover; the other new sectors measure open.

Real-browser checks and render reviews are recorded locally under
`.cache/heritage-districts/` and `.cache/verticality-review/`. The browser smokes
use actual input for deployment, movement, firing, stamp collection and driving,
and inspect the 390 px mobile layout. They block paid service endpoints.
