# Coast and east district review — 2026-09-25

These are compressed playable scenes, not surveyed models. Eight Google Street
View previews were captured and visually reviewed on 25 September 2026. Five
were accepted for scoped visible features; three were rejected because they
missed the requested target or showed obsolete context. Source dates and review
records live beside each image. `referenceFeatures` lists only features supported
by accepted previews, not complete district reconstruction.

## Remaining limitations

The street grids, relative distances and several generic secondary buildings
remain compressed interpretations. Geylang still combines the central lorongs
and Geylang Serai too closely. Sentosa's shore, resort and fort are compressed
around a driving loop; this pass does not establish geographically correct
positions across the whole island. Changi's terminal/airfield context is still
schematic. HarbourFront's industrial wharf is a gameplay interpretation, not a
claim about current port operations. The preview plans target exteriors; the rejected results below show why nearest-panorama selection still requires review.

Image-led follow-up added the Vivo rails/pergola, Palawan twin lookout towers,
Changi tower radome/glazing strip and Geylang air-conditioning/awnings. Browser
review also exposed a reversed Jewel panel slope: it produced large roof gaps
and a dark interior. The roof slopes now follow their radial endpoints and the
glazing is translucent. The Sentosa overview also showed the lagoon hidden beneath its sand base; its water and ripple surfaces now sit visibly above the sand. Ground collision and roads are unchanged by these facade refinements.
