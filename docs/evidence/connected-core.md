# Connected core district revision

This revision replaces the isolated elevated routes in Orchard, Queenstown,
Raffles Place and Marina Bay. It changes existing building volumes into usable
space, rather than placing walkable slabs beside sealed building meshes.

These are authored, compressed game interiors and circulation arrangements.
The existing reviewed exterior motifs remain feature-level references; the new
interiors, room arrangements and inter-building links were not surveyed or
captured from Google. No new reference calls were made.

## Orchard

ION, Wisma and Ngee Ann's retail podium masses are removed. Their recognisable
upper forms remain. Two elevated gallery rings surround each open atrium at
5 and 10 metres, with retail counters/bays, multiple ground approaches and
stairs through the void. The rear links connect different mall levels: reaching
the eastern continuation requires moving through Wisma's upper circulation.
Ground arcades and atrium sightlines sit below the same occupied galleries.
This is an authored retail circuit, not a claim that these exact links exist.

## Queenstown

The northern paired housing blocks retain upper residential forms while their
lower levels become actual community floors at 3.4 and 7.4 metres. Room dividers
leave circulation on both sides. Galleries enclose the shared courtyard;
separate stairs on the eastern and western ends provide different approaches
and routes back to street level. Void decks remain usable underneath.
These compressed connections are not an exact HDB block plan.

## Raffles Place

The City Tower and Arcade podiums are opened below 17 metres. Two occupied
retail/office podium levels surround interior voids, with stairs inside each
building and links on opposite sides at different heights. The street remains
an alternative route below; the upper towers remain scenery.

## Marina Bay

Six formerly solid southern arcade buildings become ground rooms, first-floor
galleries and broad roof gardens. They form two connected three-building
courts. Four promenade stairs reach the galleries, and four separate court
stairs reach the gardens. Room partitions and roof planting divide the playable
areas without turning each roof into a thin scenic crossing. This adapts the
existing southern civic frontage; it is not labelled as an exact Marina Barrage
building reconstruction.

## Validation

The dedicated inhabited-floor tests pass for all four districts: every authored
floor has standing room and spawn-connected samples. All 25 authored stair
connections traverse in both directions with the actual walking kernel. The
mesh/body/headroom diagnostic probes initially found no warnings; those probes
sample locations and do not constitute a visual quality verdict. Root-level
render review and integration checks are recorded with the overall revision.

## Follow-up visual detailing

The first floor-level render exposed blank Queenstown room partitions and bare
Orchard counters. Queenstown now has framed door panels, handles, grilled
windows, community-room plates, skirting and ceiling lights. Orchard's retail
bays now have framed display walls, shop headers, illuminated counters and
small display stock. These details occupy existing partition/counter footprints;
no circulation or collision layout changed. Typecheck and all four direct
movement/mesh-clearance diagnostics passed again after this detailing.

The Marina roof view also exposed plain green planter boxes. They now contain
concrete troughs, soil and layered shrubs, with slatted bench seating within
the original planter footprint. Raffles podium partitions now have framed
window/door frontage, service/cafe signs and ceiling lighting. Typecheck and
all four direct clearance/movement audits passed after these additions.
