# Connected north district elevation redesign

This replaces the isolated-terrace approach with inhabited spatial networks. All dimensions and internal circulation are authored gameplay adaptations, not surveyed floor plans. Existing accepted reference features remain narrowly scoped; no new capture or imagery claim is made.

| District | Integrated playable change | Route choice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bishan | Two gallery circuits at 6.8 and 10.8 m around paired existing housing slabs; a lower court connection; open void decks | Two ground stairs, two separate inter-level stairs, clockwise/anticlockwise galleries, lower court and ground alternatives |
| Toa Payoh | Front/rear galleries and end connections around both Dragon precinct housing slabs, at 4.2 and 8.4 m; sealed service cores removed from the playable gallery edges | Independent north/south ground entries, opposite court stairs, housing circuits surrounding the existing playground |
| Woodlands | Both previously solid MSCPs rebuilt as open 4/8/12 m parking decks around central circulation wells; retained parking markings and structural columns | Two entrances per building and opposing stairs between each pair of floors; parking lanes and ground undercroft remain useful spaces |
| Punggol | Waterway mall's lower sealed podium opened into two wide retail rings at 5 and 10 m, with shop fixtures and an actual central atrium under the retained residential towers | Three ground approaches and two opposing inter-level stairs; both sides of the atrium connect front and rear |
| Upper Thomson | Three broad earth-backed forest contour shelves at 2/5/8 m with trees rooted at their actual height; sloping ground connects them | Two climb branches meet at clearings, plus northern descent before the road; the low shore boardwalk remains separate |

The old detached mall-approach terrace, Woodlands waterfront platform, Thomson neighbourhood-green deck, and Toa Payoh toy gallery were removed. Existing useful river/shore crossings were retained. The east Woodlands MSCP moved 13 m west so its usable east deck clears the adjacent housing tower.

## Verification

- Tested every new connection in both directions using the shipped movement kernel, exploration radius 0.65 m, at the centre and both lateral tracks (half-width minus 0.9 m).
- The connected-place audit reached standing probes on every authored north district floor from district spawn. It checks physical access, not design quality.
- Diagnostic mesh body/headroom probes reported no warnings for the north floors or connections.
- Existing district sector ground anchors and original bridge traversals remained clear in the focused route tests.
- Full-world visual review, sector data adjustments, and full suite results are tracked by the integrating change. This note does not assert a full browser playthrough or architectural fidelity.

During verification a housing court required wider side lanes around its stairs, and Toa Payoh planting was moved away from the outer stair edges. These changes prevent a centreline-only traversal check from hiding restricted body clearance.
