# blockplaySG promo copy

House rules for all of this: no em-dashes, warm and specific, nothing the game does not actually do.
Everything below is checked against the source. The Encik lines are quoted from `src/game/fps-callouts.ts`.

## Reel caption

We rebuilt your neighbourhood. 🏙️

Marina Bay, Queenstown and Raffles Place, remade as little 3D worlds you can walk or drive through. Stand under the block. Wait at the void deck. Find the kopi shop. Collect the orange stamps on the way.

It is called BlockPlay because that is where we all grew up: the block, the lift lobby, the void deck at 6pm.

There is also an arena mode, and Encik is on the radio. Real callouts, real kancheong: "Find cover lah! You think your ILBV is magic?"

Ryan and I built this in one day at the OpenAI Astra Hackathon. Now we need you.

Play it: blockplaysg.fun
Vote: link in bio. Voting closes 16 Sept, 11:59pm SGT. You need a ChatGPT account, 3 votes each, max 1 per project.

If you have ever lived in a block, this one is for you. 🇸🇬
