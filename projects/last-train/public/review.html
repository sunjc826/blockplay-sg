Last Train

A story you step into. An audio mystery you carry in your pocket.

A phone-first interactive fiction demo set after midnight in a fictionalized Singapore station. Listen to an ElevenLabs narrator, make a choice, and discover one of four connected endings.

Play the demo ↗Download review kit ↓

The 30-second pitch

“What if an audiobook could hear your choices? Last Train turns a short story into a place you can step into. You arrive at an empty Singapore station, get a message from your own number, and decide whether to board. ElevenLabs gives the world its voice and atmosphere; a branching story makes your decisions matter. It works on the phone in your pocket, with captions and no sign-up. Today it is one mystery. Next, the same format could power museum stories, language adventures, and interactive audio fiction.”

The prototype accepts choices through buttons. Spoken choices are a possible next step, not a current feature.

A simple live demo

  1. Open Last Train on your phone and tap Catch the last train. Let the first scene play.
  2. Tap Atmosphere off to add the generated station ambience. Use headphones if the room is noisy.
  3. Choose Step inside → Take the umbrella → Visit that rainy day. You will reach “The borrowed umbrella.”
  4. Point out that captions, playback controls, four endings, and local discovery tracking already work.
  5. Start again and choose Stay on the platform → Walk toward the exit to show a different outcome.

You can choose before a clip finishes to fit a short judging slot. Read the scene silently if audio is inconvenient.

What is implemented

  • Nine original narrated scenes, a complete branching story, and four reachable endings.
  • ElevenLabs text-to-speech and generated sound effects, cached as MP3s and delivered by Cloudflare.
  • Responsive layout, caption toggle, play/pause, seeking, optional atmosphere, and share controls.
  • Ending collection saved locally on the device. No sign-in, microphone permission, or API key in the browser.
  • A standalone static Cloudflare Worker deployment, separate from the existing Astra game.

Scope and next steps

This is a polished prototype with authored branches and pre-generated narration. It does not generate new stories, understand spoken responses, or run an ElevenLabs conversational agent. The next experiment would be spoken choices with a tap fallback, followed by a second story and listener testing.

Automated checks cover story reachability, matching narration, audio integrity, and JavaScript type checking. Browser visual and physical-phone playback review remain pending because the development Mac was locked.

Architecture

Original script → story.js (scene graph)
                      ↓
ElevenLabs TTS + SFX → once-only request cache
                      ↓
MP3s + HTML + CSS + JavaScript
                      ↓
Cloudflare Workers static assets
                      ↓
Phone browser → choices → next scene
             → local ending collection

The generation tool reuses completed audio and blocks ambiguous paid retries. Public playback makes no paid generation requests. See ElevenLabs TTS documentation.
